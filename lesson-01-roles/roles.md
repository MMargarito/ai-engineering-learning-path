Part 3:

An AI Engineer could help a fire alarm company by developing AI systems that act as a “soft check” for engineering, filing, and administrative work. These systems could verify that TM-1 forms and filing documents are complete, count devices and device types on risers, generate battery calculations and equipment counts, check fire alarm Boolean logic, and identify potential errors in filing packages.

AI agents could also assist with tasks such as looking up Building Code information, creating pricing lists, and scheduling inspections through the FDNY website.

Another useful system could track repeat deficiencies found in LODs. An AI agent could organize these issues into a CSV or JSON file and identify recurring problems. A separate “checker agent” could then warn Engineers and administrators about common mistakes before a filing is submitted. Success could be measured by tracking whether repeated deficiencies decrease over time.
